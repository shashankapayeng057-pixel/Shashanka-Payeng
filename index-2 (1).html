About Me

Allow me to introduce myself.

You may call me Shashanka Payeng. Because I love people, I became a life coach, and it's my passion to help others in times of distress. I live in a community where high value is placed on family, loyalty, kindness, and respect for others.

During my college years, I learned that people-watching was a great education in and of itself. It helped me understand that the world presents us with a wide variety of hiccups, for lack of a better term. I too have had my share of ups and downs. But I was fortunate enough to have a very wise and special person in my life to help me get through those times — it became both my inspiration and empowerment to transition into the role of life coach.

As such, I have the presence of mind to ask the right questions, to listen to problems with sensitivity, and to provide my clients with honest, helpful insight. My 40-minute session isn't guaranteed to cure your problems, but rather to arm you with some tools and thoughts to work through them for a successful outcome.

"With a life coach, you are not alone. I am available to listen."

— Shashanka Payeng

Good to Know

A few things before we begin.

  • Sessions run 40 minutes. Out of respect for everyone's time, we keep to the schedule.
  • Payment is due before your session begins.
  • Coaching isn't therapy. I can't promise a specific outcome — only my full attention, honest insight, and practical tools to help you move forward.
  • Our sessions are about you and what you're facing. I keep the focus there rather than on political or religious debate.
  • I do my best to support every client, but coaching isn't the right fit for everyone or every situation. If that's the case, I'll say so and point you toward the right kind of help.
  • If you're having thoughts of suicide or self-harm, please contact a licensed crisis service right away — in the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911 in an emergency. Life coaching is not a substitute for mental health or medical crisis care.
  • At the end of each session, I'll ask for a little feedback. It helps me keep improving — and helps others find their way here too.
